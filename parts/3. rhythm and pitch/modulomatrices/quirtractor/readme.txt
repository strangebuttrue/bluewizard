QUIRTRACTOR
===========

Quirtractor ("QUIRky atTRACTOR")is a STEPAROUND-style CV picker that routes CV from at least one of the paired LFOs to another in the form of frequency modulation. The RECIPROCAL QUIRTRACTOR sends FM from either oscillator to its counterpart, and vise-versa. A little goes a long way - low feedback values can produce complex, slowly-evolving, lively patterns. Voice them well and they sound great.

All versions include at least one PULSES for pulse division shenanigans. Layout is a little tricky because of feedback circuits - signal doesn't only flow L-R.

Showcase for the QUIRTRACTOR-R. Notice very gentle cross-modulation, low numbers on the PULSESes, and carefully staged scale/offset and logic processing on the pitch CV. This patch has been segregated into function zones, so QUIRTRACTOR-R is scattered about the patch like Osiris among the conspirators of Typhon.
