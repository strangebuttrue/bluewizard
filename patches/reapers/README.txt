TEMPLATE PATTERNER & INSTRUMENT
===============================

Some patches to load up in Reaper. There's Reaper track templates that contain these



PATTERNER
---------

Use this to crank out patterns to MIDI which you can then toss around to other tracks.

- Includes OFFSETs to convert MIDI pitch values to 0-10v CV and verse visa

- MINISTEP and a FUNC do step-around stuff 

- Another FUNC you can fun from a Reaper automation lane. Use PHASOR DIVIDE & MULTIPLY to turn this into faster and slower ramps. Lose less fidelity turning fast oscillations into slower ones. Do it the other way and a SLEW will help dispel bit depth noise.


INSTRUMENT
----------

To render MIDI to sound.

- Includes OFFSETs to convert MIDI pitch values to 0-10v CV and verse visa

- OFFSET and QUANTUM do some basic pitch selection

- THE 12 JUST TONES constrain pitches to a 3-limit set
