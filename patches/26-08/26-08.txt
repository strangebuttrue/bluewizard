DOUBLE SWOOP ADDER
==================
Two channels of GLEE pass to a (very useful) POLARITY SWITCH. 

Besides being the master pitch signal, through CMP and POLY LOGIC this gates a chord change signal to the SEQUENTIAL SWITCHs above as well as the ADDITATOR output through MIX4.

But what about the harmony? Parallel pitch signals pass through parallel COERCEs. Pitch sets come from SEQUENTIAL SWITCHs scanning four harmonies:

Dm7 - Gm7(9,11) - Cm7(9,11) - Fm7

DISTRIBUTEs focus these harmonies on the middle two; the trigger to draw a new harmony comes from an LLFO gated by the GLEE signal as described above, so we don't change harmonies mid-swell. Then there's reverb.


PHO FM BAND HUM
===============
Overall what you got here is a little hum made from a complex FM oscillation (PHO set to harmonics 9,12,14,16 - Dream House partials - modulates the PRB oscillator), then put through BAND to pinch out specific frequencies. Notice everything is hooked up to the incoming MIDI pitch, but different oscillators have different octave shifts...
